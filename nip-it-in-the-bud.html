Paying off a credit card

Nip It in the Bud

The same card balance, paid off two ways. What does paying only the minimum cost?

How to read it

  • Each vine is a credit card balance. The more wall it covers, the more is owed.
  • Interest grows it back every month, in new shoots at the tips.
  • Each payment is the gardener pruning it. The pile of clippings is the interest paid so far.

Two plans

  • Minimum payments (left): the card's minimum each month, which shrinks as the balance does.
  • A fixed payment (right): the same amount every month until the vine is gone.
  • Both cottages share one scale, so the vines and the piles compare directly.
