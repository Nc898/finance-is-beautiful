  • Each month the interest is the balance × rate ÷ 12. It is added first, then the payment comes off.
  • Owed next month = owed + interest − payment. The last payment is whatever is left.
  • The minimum due is the larger of the floor and (interest + share × balance), and never more than what is owed.
  • The fixed plan pays the larger of its fixed amount and the minimum due, because a card requires at least the minimum.
