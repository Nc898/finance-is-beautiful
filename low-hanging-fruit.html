The same pay and the same match from work. What does saving 3% instead of 6% leave behind?

How to read it

  • Each picker's own savings are golden apples, from the crates.
  • The employer's match is the farmer's red apples: half a basket for every basket saved, up to 6% of pay.
  • Every basket is planted. The orchard is the savings, and each tree grows with the years.

Two pickers

  • A saves 3% of pay (left) and picks only half the free fruit. The rest falls.
  • B saves 6% (right) and picks it all, but takes home $150 less a month.
  • Both earn the same, so the orchards compare directly.
