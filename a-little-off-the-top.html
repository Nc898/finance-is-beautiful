The same savings in two funds that grow alike. One charges 1% a year, the other 0.1%. What does a small fee take?

How to read it

  • Each fund is a tree: its painted crown is the money in it.
  • Every month the fee is cut off the top and stacked in the woodpile beside the tree.
  • The pale ghost around each crown is the tree the same savings would grow with no fee at all.

Two funds

  • Both start with $10,000 and save $500 a month for 40 years, growing 7% a year.
  • Fund A (left) charges 1% a year. Fund B (right) charges 0.1%, as many index funds do.
  • Both trees are the same tree, so they compare directly.
