Snowball or avalanche

On a Roll

Three debts and one monthly budget. Which should you pay off first?

How to read it

  • Each snowball is a debt. Its size is what you owe on it.
  • Falling snow is interest: it settles on each ball at that debt's rate, most on the highest.
  • Each month both hills pay every minimum, then put what is left of the budget on one ball. That ball is rolled down to the finish line as it is paid off.

Two orders

  • Snowball (left) pays off the smallest debt first, for quick wins.
  • Avalanche (right) pays off the highest rate first, which costs the least interest.
  • When a debt is cleared, its payment rolls on to the next one.
