How this is calculated

At Jan 1980

Basket = each quantity × its price, added up. Minutes of work = basket ÷ (weekly pay ÷ 40) × 60.

    Sources

      • Average prices show the price level in a month, not the exact rate of change. The yearly rises come from the consumer price index.
      • A week's pay is the median usual weekly earnings of full-time workers, before tax. A quarter's figure covers its three months.
      • 1980 is where these average prices begin. It was the peak of a high-inflation era, so it is not a typical starting line.

      Bread and Butter

      A real week's groceries and a real week's pay, every month since 1980.

      How to read it

      • The chalkboard shows what the basket costs. The grocer writes each month's prices by hand.
      • The pay envelope holds a median week's pay.
      • The headline is how many minutes of that pay the basket takes.

      Where it starts

      • 1980 was the peak of a high-inflation era: prices were rising 14% a year, and pay had just fallen behind.
      • Real prices and pay from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.
