About

Hello all and thank you for taking the time to check out this website! My name is Nick Childs and I am a college student working towards a career in data analytics. I am passionate about personal finance and building fun, informative websites so I thought this would be a great way to combine these passions into something that could help people understand core personal finance concepts.

I believe that learning through interesting and accurate demonstrations can often stick with us longer than simply reading words from a page. My goal for these demos is to create something interactive where viewers can watch changes in real-time with the ability to drag the scene back and forth through time.

These demos use real numbers and formulas to create simple models with steady rates to keep concepts clear and understandable. Of course, in the real world this can be much different. Demos are illustrations of concepts, not real-world predictions or advice.

This site was built using the help of AI. All demos and math concepts were checked by hand for accuracy. Each demo was created from my own thoughts and brought to life with AI.

If you would like to keep up with this project or any of my future work, my LinkedIn is below. I plan on creating an Instagram account showcasing this site alongside other animated personal finance topics.
